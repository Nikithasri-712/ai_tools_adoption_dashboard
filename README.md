**🤖 AI Tools Adoption Dashboard**

**Overview**

A dynamic Power BI dashboard exploring global AI adoption trends across 2023–2024. Analyze how tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude, Bard, and Stable Diffusion are being used across industries, countries, age groups, and company sizes.

**🚀 Highlights**

- Track AI adoption across 10 countries
- Compare 2023 vs 2024 growth trends
- Explore adoption by industry, age group, and company size
- Interactive slicers for deeper analysis
- KPI tracking for Adoption Rate and Daily Active Users

**📊 Key Insights**
- ChatGPT dominates with ~40% market share
- Midjourney follows with ~30% adoption
- AI adoption more than doubled from 2023 to 2024
- Strong engagement across all age groups

**🛠 Tools Used**
- Power BI
- PostgreSQL
